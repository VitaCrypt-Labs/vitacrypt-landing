The full description

One profile from your sources: lab results, a DNA file, wearable data and the air quality where you live, read together instead of one PDF at a time.

Cited or Hypothesis on every link: each connection the app draws is labelled Cited, with the papers it rests on, or Hypothesis, when the evidence is thin.

A Lab Agent that checks its own plan: it suggests habits, a retest date and questions for your clinician. When your next lab arrives, it checks whether the plan worked and keeps, swaps or asks for more of each habit. It never diagnoses and never suggests doses.

What we ask: honest reports, about 10 minutes a week. Tell us what broke, what confused you and what was missing. This is an early-stage MVP built by one person, so expect rough edges. Your reports decide what gets fixed first.
