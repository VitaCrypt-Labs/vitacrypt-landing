01 · What you get

One profile, and an agent that follows up

  • One profile from your sourcesLab results, a DNA file, wearable data and the air quality where you live, read together instead of one PDF at a time.
  • Cited or Hypothesis on every linkEach connection the app draws is labelled: Cited, with the papers it rests on, or Hypothesis, when the evidence is thin.
  • A Lab Agent that checks its own planIt suggests habits, a retest date and questions for your clinician. When your next lab arrives, it checks whether the plan worked and keeps, swaps or asks for more of each habit. It never diagnoses and never suggests doses.

02 · What we ask

Honest reports, about 10 minutes a week

Tell us what broke, what confused you and what was missing. This is an early-stage MVP built by one person, so expect rough edges. Your reports decide what gets fixed first.

03 · What leaves the app

What the Lab Agent sends, and to whom

  • PubMed, Europe PMCResearch databasesGene, marker and condition names, as search terms.
  • Anthropic (Claude)AI modelCoded facts, such as "LDL: high, rising", gene names, variant IDs and your genotype letters, without your name or email. No files, no exact values.
  • Air quality, and what you tapEverything elseAir-quality services get your location rounded to about 1 km. Anything more, such as a lab order or a link for your clinician, is sent only when you tap an action that says what it will send.

Each Lab Agent send is logged in the app under "What left VitaCrypt". Some older parts of the app still send more than the Lab Agent; they are listed, with everything else, on What happens to your data, and we fix them before the first tester uploads a lab.

04 · Who we invite first

People who test their blood regularly

We start with people who have a blood test from the last year and expect another one soon, because only they can see the agent check its own plan. A DNA file helps too. If you have none of these yet, apply anyway: we invite you once the app is useful without labs.

We invite in small groups, five people first, and email you if you are invited. We cannot promise a date.
