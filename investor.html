What happens to your data

Safety by construction, in full

Fixed agent. Seven fixed steps and a fixed tool list in code. Fixed rules decide what the agent does; the model judges papers and words the plan. An output guard blocks doses, medication changes and diagnoses.

What leaves. The Lab Agent's seven steps send coded fields only to the model (Anthropic's Claude): the Lab Agent never receives a name, email, file or exact value. Searches carry gene and marker names only. Every send is logged in the app. Lab reading is a separate call: the report text goes to Claude with lines that identify the person removed, and the removal can miss an unlabelled name. Older insight cards send a fuller profile.

Who is ahead of us, in full. ChatGPT Health on reach; Function Health on capital and its own lab panels, valued at $2.5B after a $298M Series B (TechCrunch, 19 Nov 2025); VitaDAO's Vita App on device connections, open sign-up and self-reported user-held keys. Based on public pages and our own review, Nov 2026.

Cited or Hypothesis. Every link is checked against PubMed and Europe PMC by an AI judge; no invented PMIDs. Clinical topics become questions for your clinician.

Acts, after a tap. Retest reminders, a clinician link, labs nearby and a lab order (US sandbox), each previewed and started by the person.

Limits we state. Data is encrypted at rest under a per-user key, but the server can unwrap it to run the agent; client-held keys are roadmap. Zero data retention terms are requested from the model provider, not confirmed.
