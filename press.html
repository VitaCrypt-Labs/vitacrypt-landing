About 50 words

VitaCrypt is a personal health profile: it joins DNA, blood tests, wearable readings, microbiome, check-ins and local air quality and checks every link against published research. Its Lab Agent reads each new lab, plans habits, repeat tests and clinician questions, and at the next lab checks whether that plan worked.

About 120 words

VitaCrypt is an early-stage personal health MVP built by solo founder Paul Burg. It reads DNA, blood tests, microbiome reports, questionnaires, wearable readings and local air quality as one profile. Each link it finds is checked against PubMed and Europe PMC and labelled Cited or Hypothesis. The Lab Agent follows up on every new blood test in seven fixed steps: it compares the lab with earlier ones, links changes to the person's DNA, searches the research and shows every query, then plans habits, repeat tests and questions for a clinician. At the next lab it checks its own plan against the habit log and keeps, swaps or retires each habit. It gives no diagnoses and never sets a dose. A guided demo with synthetic data needs no sign-up.

Full wording: privacy and limits

The Lab Agent's seven steps send coded facts only to the language model, never a name, file or exact values. Reading an uploaded lab is a separate step: the report text goes to Anthropic's Claude with lines we recognise as personal removed. Values, sex, age, test date and lab name stay, and the removal can miss a name printed without a label.

Insight cards send a fuller profile than the Lab Agent: lab and body values, DNA variants, symptoms, questionnaire scores, sex and a 5-year age band, but no name, email, city or coordinates. Older insight cards may send more.

Health data is encrypted at rest under a per-user key held by our server. The server unwraps that key to run the agent; client-held keys are roadmap. Deleting data destroys that key. Zero data retention terms are requested from the model provider, not confirmed.

Supplements appear only as official reference values with their source, never as a dose.
