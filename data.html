What happens to your data

What the app keeps, what leaves it and to whom, how to erase it, and what is not finished. Early-stage beta, not medical advice.

Describes the app at app.vitacrypt.xyz as of 26 November 2026.

Storage

Stored encrypted, with a key made for you

Our server holds the key. This is not end-to-end encryption: the software, and Paul as the operator, can technically read your data. Keys held only on your device are on the roadmap.

Encrypted under your key

  • DNA results
  • Lab results
  • Questionnaires
  • Daily check-ins
  • Wearable and body values
  • Health profile and score history
  • Reasons shown with habits

Stored without encryption

  • Account email
  • DNA summary score and variant count
  • Air readings and history for your area
  • Habit names
  • File names
  • Tests ordered and repeat dates
  • Type and date of each record
  • The log of what left the app

Server in France (EU). Text PDFs, scans and photos are read on our server; cloud text recognition is off. Uploaded files are not kept, only the values read from them.

Also kept

Your email and a hashed password (we never see the password itself). Which version of the privacy notice you accepted, and when. How you use the app, without health values: sign-up, opening the app, adding a kind of data, viewing insights, running the Lab Agent, and your ratings of cards. Names of test results we do not support yet, counted across all users without values or senders.

Your sex, birth year and height, if you give them or they are printed on what you add; each shows where it came from in Profile, where you can correct it. Apple Health data you added before 17 November 2026 stays in your account until you delete it; the import was removed that day.

Some things are stored without encryption: your account email, the summary score from your DNA file and how many variants it contained, air-quality readings and history for your area, the habits you adopt (the habit text, not the reasons shown with it), file names, which tests you ordered and when repeat tests are due, the type and date of each record, and the log of what left the app.

The log

“What left VitaCrypt”

Profile, then “What left VitaCrypt” lists each send: when, where, which kinds of fields.

What the log holds

Sends from these features are written to a log in the app. The log names the fields, never their values. It shows the latest 200 entries. Some literature searches made for insight cards are not logged yet.

Deletion

Deleting your data destroys your key

  1. Open Profile

    Profile, then “Permanently delete my health data”.

  2. Confirm

    Your health records and Lab Agent data are deleted, including the log.

  3. Your key is destroyed

    What was encrypted with it can no longer be read, by anyone, including us.

This cannot be undone.

Habits you added yourself stayCopies already sent stayYour sign-in account stays
What stays, and how to get a copy

Once older backups are deleted, anything encrypted with that key can no longer be read, by anyone, including us. Copies already sent to others, such as a lab order or a clinician link someone has opened, are not affected.

Your sign-in account stays. To have it removed as well, write to office@vitacrypt.xyz.

Before you erase, Profile, then “Download my data” gives you everything we store for your account as one JSON file.

Not finished

What is not finished

Older insight cards send more. They still send Claude lab and body values, DNA variants, symptoms and questionnaire scores, a fuller profile than the Lab Agent sends.

Backups outlive an erase. Backups made before you erase still hold your data, encrypted, for up to 35 days.
