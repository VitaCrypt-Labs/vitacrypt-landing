What happens to your data

What the VitaCrypt app keeps, what leaves it and to whom, how to erase it, and what is not finished yet. VitaCrypt is an early-stage MVP and not medical advice.

Describes the app at app.vitacrypt.xyz as of 29 October 2026.

01 · What we keep

Stored encrypted, with a key made for you

Your health data is stored on our server: DNA results, lab results, questionnaires, daily check-ins, wearable values and your health profile. It is encrypted with a key made for you.

Our server holds that key, so the app can read your data to build your insights. This is not end-to-end encryption: we are able to read your data, and we do not claim otherwise.

Some things are stored without encryption: your account email, the summary score from your DNA file and how many variants it contained, air-quality readings and history for your area, your habits and the reasons shown with them, file names, which tests you ordered and when repeat tests are due, the type and date of each record, and the log of what left the app.

Lab files are read on our server. Reading photos or scans with a cloud service is switched off, so the app asks for a PDF that contains text.

03 · The log

"What left VitaCrypt"

Sends from these features are written to a log in the app. Open Profile, then "What left VitaCrypt", to see when something was sent, where it went and which kinds of fields it carried.

The log names the fields, never their values. It shows the latest 200 entries. Some literature searches made for insight cards are not logged yet.

04 · Erasing

Deleting your data destroys your key

In Profile, "Permanently delete my health data" destroys your key and deletes your health records and Lab Agent data, including the log. Once older backups are deleted, anything encrypted with that key can no longer be read, by anyone, including us. This cannot be undone.

Habits you added yourself stay. Copies already sent to others, such as a lab order or a clinician link someone has opened, are not affected.

Your sign-in account stays. To have it removed as well, write to privacy@vitacrypt.xyz.

05 · Not done yet

What is not finished

  • The older insight cards still send Claude a fuller profile than the Lab Agent does: lab values, DNA findings, microbiome, wearable and lifestyle numbers, questionnaire scores, check-in summaries, habits, local air and weather, and your city name. Emails, phone numbers and dates are filtered out.
  • Server backups made before you erase your data still hold your key in protected form, and the unencrypted items listed above, until those backups are deleted.
